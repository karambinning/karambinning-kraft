Upstairs

What we pour, and why.

There wasn't really specialty coffee in Wakefield when we opened. So we set out to build the kind of room we'd want to drink in: high-end, considered, not a chain copy.

We pour beans from North Star Coffee Roasters in Leeds — chosen for the cup and the fair-wages economics behind it. Brazil for the espresso, Kenya for the filter, rotating guest origins through the week.

The kit is a Modbar — espresso modules sunk into the bar rather than perched on top of it. Less spectacle, more eye-contact across the counter, and a brew that's the same on the first shot as it is on the four-hundredth.
